# atc-web production suite 1.28.0

Release date: 2026-10-16

All 22 repositories at `v1.28.0`: 20 HTTP services, `atc-service-core` and `atc-stack`. The installer
descriptor is [`../installation-manifests/v1.28.0.json`](../installation-manifests/v1.28.0.json),
selected by [`../installation-manifests/supported.json`](../installation-manifests/supported.json).

## atc-notify

- Suppression list (hard bounce, complaint, soft bounce after `SOFT_BOUNCE_LIMIT`, unsubscribe,
  manual). Suppressed recipients are dropped on accept and listed in `suppressed`; a message with no
  recipient left answers `422 RECIPIENTS_SUPPRESSED`. The worker checks again before sending.
- Bounce and complaint intake: `POST /v1/suppressions/events` (admin key) or the HMAC-signed
  `POST /v1/hooks/bounces`.
- One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) when `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` and `UNSUBSCRIBE_SECRET` are set.
- Send quotas: `DAILY_SEND_LIMIT`, `KEY_DAILY_LIMIT`, `RECIPIENT_HOURLY_LIMIT` (default 20); over the
  limit answers `429 QUOTA_EXCEEDED`.

## atc-search

- Per-index `language: "tr"` enables rule-based Turkish stemming; changing it reindexes in place.
- Published atc-content releases are mirrored into `content-<ns>` indexes through
  `content.release.*` events, or on demand with `POST /v1/sync/content/:ns`.

## Other services

- atc-export: sources `atc-notify` and `atc-notify-suppressions`.
- atc-console: Notify suppressions page; language field on the search index form.
- atc-stack: search/content/webhook-out/export wiring; MCP notify key has a role; 157 MCP tools.
